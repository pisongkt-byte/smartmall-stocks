Rewards & Risks

Rewards

Retention Rate 95.3% overall (Index segment 97.5%) — the highest Q2 figure on record, and improving YoY in 3 of 4 segments
BlackRock re-signed the ETF index license through 31 March 2035 (27 Jan 2026 8-K) — the single client with the strongest incentive to self-index just locked in for 9 more years
Multiple compression is real and large: P/E 40.5x -> 29.6x (-27%), EV/EBITDA 31.2x -> 22.5x (-28%) since end-2023, while adjusted EPS is up +37.6% over the same stretch
Index segment — the core moat, 58.9% of revenue, 76.2% adjusted EBITDA margin — is accelerating (+17.2% Run Rate), not decelerating
Negative book equity keeps it off most quantitative screens, which is exactly the kind of overlooked setup Michael Burry has historically hunted (though his actual MSCI position was never confirmed — Scion's final 13F, Q3 2025, held no MSCI, and Scion deregistered with the SEC on 10 Nov 2025, so no future 13F can ever confirm it, `[unverified]`)

Risks

Rout risk is live: S&P 500/Dow in correction, CME FedWatch ~100% for a 9 Dec hike; a deeper equity drawdown hits ABF (~27% of revenue, record ~$1B run rate) and the multiple at once — bear $520 (−15%), worse in a 25% AUM drop.
Sustainability & Climate segment (10.6% of revenue) is genuinely deteriorating: net new recurring sales -62% YoY to $1.9M, cancellations +40%, retention -1.5pp, CFO guiding roughly zero-to-negative net new sales for two more quarters
Still the most expensive name in its own duopoly on EV/EBITDA — MSCI 22.5x vs SPGI 16.9x, a ~33% premium even after the de-rate
Chief Accounting Officer seat vacant since 9 Aug 2026 (C. Jack Read departed on schedule) with no successor named 7 weeks on; CFO Wiechmann covers as interim principal accounting officer with Kristine Johnson as interim Global Controller — a governance yellow flag for a company running negative book equity
Interest expense jumped to $71.0M in Q2'26 (+54% YoY) as old 3.25-4.00% notes rolled into new 5.15-5.25% notes — a ~$74M/yr permanent earnings headwind
Effective ETF basis-point fee down -6.0% YoY; a second BlackRock fee-reduction tranche takes effect 1 Jan 2027, and other fund issuers will use that deal as a negotiating benchmark
Asset-based fees (26.9% of revenue) are directly tied to global ETF AUM ($2,818B) — a 20-25% equity-market drawdown would hit this revenue line immediately with no contractual floor
Buyback pace roughly halved in 1H2026 (~2.7% annualized vs FY2025's ~4.4%) as net leverage nears the self-imposed 3x cap
No Thai DR exists for MSCI — buying it requires opening an offshore brokerage account, which is not worth doing for this position alone
