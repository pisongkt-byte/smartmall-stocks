Rewards & Risks

Rewards

Genuine structural growth story: revenue CAGR ~23.6%/yr over 5 years, net margin expanded from 7.9% to 12.5%, and Q2/2026 revenue still grew 46.5% YoY despite the profit miss — the AI/data-center power-electronics demand is real, not hype-only.
Exceptionally strong balance sheet: net cash position (฿18.2B cash vs ฿4.28B debt, D/E 4.06%), positive FCF, self-funded capacity expansion (new ฿3,200M Bangpu factory) with zero incremental leverage.
Stock has already corrected -27.4% from its 52-week high (฿372 → ฿270), giving some cushion versus the peak-hype entry point, even though the multiple is still very rich in absolute terms.
Sell-side is broadly bullish: 19 analysts average "Buy" with consensus target ฿333.37 (+23.5%), high target ฿440 from Bualuang Securities (2027 reference).
ROE 31.5% and consistently improving margins over 5 years show real operating leverage, not just revenue growth funded by dilution or debt.

Risks

VALUATION IS EXTREME RELATIVE TO A RISK THAT JUST MATERIALIZED. Trailing P/E 112x and forward P/E 68-80x price in years of uninterrupted hypergrowth. Q2/2026 just proved growth CAN stumble — net profit missed broker estimates by ~30% and fell -33.1% QoQ on a raw-material shortage (high-voltage MOSFETs, memory-related components). At this multiple, any further disappointment gets punished far harder than at a normal stock's valuation.

Nearly all growth is tied to one theme: global AI/data-center hyperscaler capex. Any pause or "digestion" period in hyperscaler AI infrastructure spending (a pattern seen before in prior tech capex cycles) would hit DELTA's revenue directly and fast — unlike a utility or consumer staple with sticky demand.
Management itself flags tariff and energy-cost headwinds as ongoing margin pressures on top of the component shortage — multiple simultaneous cost pressures, not a single isolated issue.
New ฿3,200M factory capex (2026-2027) will pressure FCF during the build period even though it's funded from operating cash flow — worth watching for any change in that funding plan if margins stay under pressure longer than guided.
