Risks

CASH CONTENT SPEND vs AMORTIZATION HAS FLIPPED: H1'26 additions to content assets $9,774M (+32.3% YoY) vs amortization $8,529M (+11.4%) = ratio 1.146x, ABOVE the ~1.1x FY guide (H1'25 was 0.965x). And Netflix is cutting the engagement report from bi-annual to ANNUAL starting 2027: second transparency downgrade in two years, arriving exactly when the market needs the check
Second board departure in ~2 months: Anne Sweeney resigned 26 Jul 2026 (8-K item 5.02, no stated disagreement), following Reed Hastings' full board exit. Stacked on the WBD → NBCU → Lionsgate → Letterboxd M&A pattern that drove Warren's Trim call

Op-margin ceiling near 33%: Q2'26 actual 33.4%, Q3 guide 33.2%, yet FY guide held at 31.5% for a second quarter — management expects H2 give-back. The +200bps/yr expansion era looks over
