Risks

SSSG MAY BE STRUCTURALLY STUCK NEAR ZERO — this is the whole bear case. FY2025 SSSG was −0.3% (first non-COVID negative ever) and customers/store/day have gone 978 → 951 → 958 over two years. Revenue is being held up by ticket size (฿85 → ฿90) and food mix, both of which have ceilings. If SSSG stays 0–1% forever, the fair multiple is 11–12x, not 16–18x, and the stock is worth ฿38–42 — a 12–21% loss from ฿48.
'ไทยช่วยไทยพลัส' 60/40 co-pay scheme (1 Jun – 30 Sep 2026) explicitly excludes franchise convenience stores from registering as merchants. That is state money routed deliberately AWAY from 7-Eleven and into small shops — Q2 takes one month of it, Q3 takes the full quarter. Expect the Q3 print (~Nov 2026) to be ugly.
Goodwill ฿363.7B exceeds total equity ฿343.4B — tangible equity is negative ~฿20B. A Lotus's impairment (its profit IS shrinking) would hit the ≤2.0x net-debt/adjusted-equity covenant and force a TRIS review.
CPAXT is bleeding: Q2/2026 net profit ฿1,874M −18% YoY, GPM 14.3% → 14.1%, opex +7.2% on energy and last-mile; H1 −5.3%; Makro SSSG −1%, Lotus's SSSG −4%. UOB Kay Hian cut CPAXT to HOLD, TP ฿14.50. CPALL owns ~60%.
CP Group already TRIED to move cash-generative subsidiaries out of CPALL in 2026. The Bank of Thailand left alternative routes open (partial transfer, reduced shareholding, licence return) — a smaller version that avoids a shareholder vote is still possible.
Thai macro is the worst in ASEAN: household debt 86.7% of GDP, 2026 GDP growth forecast ~1.6%, headline inflation negative. Defensive does not mean immune — FY2025 SSSG proved that.
Cost squeeze in Q2–Q3: Middle East oil prices raise DC logistics cost and electricity is a heavy input for a cold-chain store network. CPAXT has already confirmed this hit.
Overseas expansion is failing quietly — Cambodia went from 112 to 63 stores in a single quarter (−49). Do not pay for international optionality.
