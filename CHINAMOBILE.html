What It Does
The world's largest mobile carrier — ~1 billion mobile subscribers and ~315M wireline-broadband homes (~50% of China's market). State-controlled (China Mobile Communications Group owns ~70%), it sells personal mobile/data plans, home broadband bundles, and a fast-growing enterprise 'DICT' stack (cloud, computing, AI, IoT). Service revenue is ~85% of the top line; the rest is low-margin handset/product sales.
How it makes money
Personal Market~47%Mobile voice + 5G data plans for ~1B subscribers — the mature cash engine, flat-to-low growth
Gov't & Enterprise (DICT)~22%Cloud, computing, AI, IoT, private 5G — the growth driver; computing services RMB89.8B (+11%)
Home Market~14%Wireline broadband + smart-home bundles; ~315M households, +7% YoY
Product sales~17%Handset / device resale — low-margin pass-through, not a profit driver

Rewards & Risks

Rewards

Cheap for the quality — ~11.5x FY26E earnings / ~1.0x book / ~6.7% yield, net cash RMB178.5B (Interim Report 30-Jun-26)
Dividend well covered: TTM FCF yield 8.0% > cash dividend yield; capex cut 9.5% frees cash
VAT hit is a level reset, not a market-share loss — 5G users 702M and broadband 342M still growing; HSBC rates China Mobile least impacted of the three
DICT/computing/AI is the bright spot (computing power spend >37% of capex)
Utility-like downside: price near the 52w low (HK$75.55) with a ~7% yield floor

Risks

EARNINGS NOW FALLING (new, biggest): 9M26 profit −7.9%, Q3 −12.5% after the 1-Jan-2026 VAT rise (6%→9% on data/SMS/broadband). If tariffs can't be repriced, FY27 profit stays on the lower base and a 75% payout means flat/lower DPS. Layered on SOE governance — the state could still direct the cash pile to low-return investment
GEOPOLITICS: US ADRs delisted 2021; China Mobile entities sit on the Pentagon 1260H list (June 2026 update to 188 companies, DoD procurement ban effective 30-Jun-26). The 1260H list itself has no investment ban, but bills (H.R.7075 / S.3640) would auto-align it with Treasury's NS-CMIC investor ban — status unconfirmed. Thai DR CHMOBILE19 is thin (~฿1M/day)
Structurally flat core: the personal/mobile market is saturated and price-competitive among the three state carriers; DICT is real but too small to re-accelerate a RMB1.05T top line — capital appreciation likely lags the dividend, so most of the return is the ~6% yield
