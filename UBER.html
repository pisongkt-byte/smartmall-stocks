What It Does
The world's largest mobility + delivery platform — a two-sided marketplace matching riders and eaters with drivers and couriers across 70+ countries (208M monthly users, 3.9B trips/quarter). Now a $10B+ free-cash-flow machine; acquiring Delivery Hero (€41.50/sh, $14.8B, close H2 2027) and positioning as the demand aggregator for 120,000 committed autonomous vehicles.
