Risks

THE ENTIRE EARNINGS SURGE IS A TEMPORARY OIL-PRICE WINDFALL, NOT STRUCTURAL IMPROVEMENT — AND IT HAS GOTTEN BIGGER, NOT SMALLER. The Strait of Hormuz has now been closed 211+ days; Iran's 7-day phased reopening offer (25 Sep 2026) was rejected by Trump, and Brent has climbed further to ~$103-110/bbl (from ~$91 in H1). FY2024 and FY2025 net profit were flat-to-declining BEFORE this shock. The longer and higher this runs, the deeper the eventual compression in E&P and refining margins when the strait does reopen and Brent reverts toward the $65-75 range most analysts still expect.
Price just made a NEW 52-week high (฿43.00 vs prior high ฿40.75) and now sits AT the top edge of the ฿36-43 normalized fair-value band, with updated broker targets (avg ~฿44) barely above spot. Margin of safety has shrunk versus the last review (was ฿40.50 vs ฿36-43), not improved — buying strength at the peak of an oil-shock cycle remains the worst entry setup.
Parent-company credit outlook is confirmed negative: Moody's rates PTT Baa1 senior unsecured but revised the outlook to NEGATIVE from stable in April 2025, directly tied to Thailand's sovereign outlook — a new, previously unconfirmed risk that widens the SOE-policy-risk case below.
State-enterprise policy risk: the Thai government capped retail diesel at ฿29.94-30/litre via the Oil Fuel Fund (subsidy up to ฿18.31/litre), which ran a >฿12B deficit by mid-March 2026 burning ~$32M/day. PTT itself absorbed ฿8.5B of 'government-mandated energy subsidies' plus hedging margin calls in H1/2026 — as a 51%-MOF-owned SOE, PTT's windfall profit is a standing target for redirection toward consumer relief.
Q2/2026 booked a ฿21.7B inventory loss, showing how exposed quarterly results are to crude price direction — with Brent now more volatile at higher levels, this swing risk is if anything larger heading into Q3.
Petrochemical & Refining (PTTGC/IRPC) only just turned profitable after a loss-making FY2025 and remains structurally pressured by Chinese oversupply — a fast Hormuz resolution could send this segment back into losses quickly, unwinding one of the two current growth drivers.
The 238.66M-share treasury sale (28 Sep-12 Oct 2026) lands right around the 7 Oct XD date — brokers call the price impact limited given the small 0.84% float, but the timing overlap could add short-term selling pressure on top of normal post-XD drift.
