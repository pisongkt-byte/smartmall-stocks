Risks

Growth stalled AND profit is actively shrinking — Q1'26 rev +3.4%, adj net −26%; no Q2'26 print yet to confirm stabilization
Perennial #2 to a bigger, better-funded Douyin/ByteDance — margins compressing while still growing = eroding moat, not a toll bridge
Live-streaming (~25% of revenue) in structural decline (−13.5%)
AI-video is a cash-burning, commoditizing arms race (Google Veo price-cutting) — the $18B Kling mark is a single strategic-investor-priced round, not a hard floor
NEW: Tencent cut its Kuaishou stake from 15.68% to 9.37% (HK$12.5B block trade, 2026-07-06) — removes a large strategic anchor holder, adds float
China-VIE / Thai-DR ownership overhang — you own a claim on a contract, not the assets; regulatory + geopolitical common-mode risk
Portfolio concentration: the book is already saturated with China-internet beta (BABA80, JD80, MEITUAN, XIAOMI, BIDU, TENCENT80) — Kuaishou adds the same risk, not diversification
DR KUAISH01 conversion ratio (~1:75) + liquidity thin (10-day avg ~42k units vs 3-month ~579k) — unverified on the Bualuang factsheet
