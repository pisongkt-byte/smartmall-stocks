Revenue Mix Trend
Revenue mix (US$B) — Advertising vs Other (FoA) vs Reality Labs, FY2021–FY2025. Source: Meta 10-K disaggregation (verified; Advertising = Family of Apps − Other revenue).
Advertising is ~97–98% of revenue every single year — Meta is essentially a one-product company. That razor-flat line is the core concentration risk: any hit to ad demand or ad targeting (privacy rules, a weak ad market, regulation) flows almost undiluted to the top line. 'Other' (WhatsApp Business, Meta Verified) is finally scaling ($0.7B → $2.6B) but is still a rounding error, and Reality Labs revenue has gone nowhere (~$2B) while burning ~$19B/yr in operating losses. Ads pay for the metaverse bet.
