Rewards & Risks

Rewards

Trailing P/E ~15.1x is the cheapest multiple in at least 10 years for an 89.2% gross-margin business still growing revenue 12-13% YoY
Creative & Marketing Professionals subscription revenue — the exact segment the market believes generative AI is commoditizing — ACCELERATED from +11% (Q4 FY25) to +13% YoY (Q2 FY26)
5yr revenue CAGR +13.0%, no visible fracture through either the Midjourney (2022) or ChatGPT image-gen (2025) launches
$26.78B buyback authorization remaining (26% of market cap) — even at zero revenue growth, buyback alone can drive ~11%/yr EPS growth at FY25 pace
AI-first ARR >$500M and tripling YoY, Firefly ARR ~$300M (+~50% QoQ) — early but real monetization, plus 90M freemium MAU as a future funnel
No dividend withholding tax for Thai-DR holders since Adobe pays no dividend — 100% of any gain reaches the investor

Risks

No permanent CEO and no permanent CFO simultaneously — Narayen announced departure 2026-03-12 (still no successor named as of 2026-08-15, 5+ months later) and CFO Durn left 2026-06-15 (interim). This is happening during the largest strategic pivot (freemium) since the 2013 subscription transition, and the board is paying retention bonuses to keep other executives from leaving too
Pricing power has cracked, and it's measurable, not speculative — management explicitly deferred a planned Creative Cloud H2 price increase (~$240M of ARR), directly stated on the Q2 FY26 earnings call. In a SaaS business with a real moat, annual price increases are automatic; when a company has to 'defer' one, the moat is leaking
Organic ARR growth has decelerated for 4 straight quarters (12.1% → 11.7% → 11.5% → ~8.3%), and Semrush M&A ($1.87B) papers over roughly half of the headline growth number
Disclosure quality dropped exactly when investors need more detail — Adobe collapsed 3 reporting segments into 1 starting Q1 FY26, eliminating Digital Media ARR, Digital Experience revenue, and net-new ARR by segment, even though executive pay is still tied to a 'Net New Sales Goal' the company no longer discloses
Margin direction is down 3 quarters running (non-GAAP op margin 45.5% → 44.5% actual → 44.0% Q3 guide) as S&M spend (+15.9% YoY) outgrows revenue (+12.7%), and FY25 buyback ($11.28B) exceeded FCF ($9.85B), flipping the balance sheet from net cash to net debt at a poor average purchase price (~$276 in H1 FY26 vs a $190 low)
Stock already up +38.9% from the 2026-06-25 low of $190.12 — the genuinely cheap window was $193-225 in Jun-Jul; sell-side mean target (~$264-270) is roughly at the current price and Morgan Stanley downgraded to Underweight (PT $240) on 2026-07-21 citing this exact combination of dual leadership vacancy + freemium pivot + AI spend
