Rewards & Risks

Rewards

Fortress balance sheet: CAR 19.24% (~2x minimum), NPL flat at 3.18% with 173.9% coverage — one of the safest big banks in Thailand.
Structural dividend policy upgrade (floor 25%→≥50%, target 50–60%) already delivering — FY2025 paid out 67.43%, current yield ~4.9–5.7%.
Non-interest income growing +31% YoY (wealth/insurance/investment) is genuinely offsetting NII compression, unlike a one-off windfall.
LINE BK digital-lending JV growing fast (+20-30% target FY26) — embedded optionality even if still small relative to the group.
Analyst consensus 'Strong Buy', 21 analysts, average target ฿252.51–259.46.

Risks

NIM COMPRESSION IS THE CORE THESIS RISK. NII -9.55% YoY H1/2026 on NIM falling from 3.36% to 2.91% as the Bank of Thailand cuts rates. Q2 net profit was already -9.68% QoQ — the first sign the non-interest-income offset may not keep pace every quarter. If the rate-cutting cycle continues without a clear trough, NII keeps shrinking and the stock's 'cash cow' profile depends increasingly on a more volatile non-interest income line.
P/BV has re-rated from ~0.65x in May 2026 to ~1.0x now — a very fast move that has already priced in most of the known good news (dividend policy, capital strength). Buying at ฿247, right near the 52-week/all-time high of ฿254 set just two weeks earlier, offers little margin of safety if sentiment reverses.
Thailand's elevated household debt and slow-growth macro backdrop is a sector-wide overhang for all big Thai banks — credit quality could deteriorate faster than the current benign 3.18% NPL suggests if the economy weakens.
Operating expenses grew +7.63% QoQ in Q2/2026 — worth watching whether this is a one-off (marketing/K WEALTH campaign spend) or the start of cost-to-income drift higher.
LINE BK's loan book, while growing fast percentage-wise, is still under 1.5% of KBANK's total assets — not yet a material profit driver despite the headline growth rate.
