Rewards & Risks

Rewards

Clean balance sheet vs sector peers — net debt/EBITDA just 1.1x, mostly zero-coupon converts, no near-term maturity wall
Duopoly position intact — DraftKings + FanDuel = 78% of US sports-betting GGR, and both shrank by similar amounts in Q2 (confirms the pressure is sector-wide, not share loss to each other)
Predictions optionality — Railbird (CFTC-licensed) lets DKNG participate in the event-contracts market under its own roof if courts ultimately bless the exchange model; annualized volume already at $11B run-rate
MUPs (monthly unique players) still growing +9% YoY to 3.6M — the player base isn't shrinking, only revenue-per-player is

Risks

NEW since last review (Aug 2026): DKNG itself is now the defendant in 3 consumer lawsuits (Massachusetts federal x2, South Carolina state court) alleging its own Predictions product is an unlicensed sportsbook — separate from and additive to the Kalshi litigation risk
Circuit split unresolved — Third Circuit (Apr 2026) sided with the exchange model, Connecticut (Aug 2026) ruled the opposite way; heads to the Supreme Court, 1–2 year binary outcome with no clear edge either way
Core business growth is flattering itself — handle +14.5% mixes in low-margin Predictions volume; hold rate compression (8.7%→6.8%) is partly structural, not just bad luck; DKNG discloses less segment detail than FLUT, making this hard to verify independently
Permanent state tax increases already landed (NJ 14.25%→21%, LA 15%→21.5%, MD 15%→20%) — these don't reverse when the promo cycle normalizes
Negative free cash flow (H1'26 −$24M) while trading at 16.9x EV/EBITDA — paying a full multiple for a business that isn't yet generating cash
