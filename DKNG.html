Rewards & Risks

Rewards

CEO claims (unaudited, 22 Sep) sportsbook handle +15% YoY at NFL season start — a sharp reacceleration from Q2's −4.6% revenue, if it holds up in Q3's actual filing
Ninth Circuit ruling (28 Aug 2026) sided with states over Kalshi/Crypto.com/Robinhood on prediction-market contracts — a legal tailwind for DKNG's state-licensed model, even if it hasn't shown up in market share yet
Refinancing (25 Aug 2026) repurchased part of the dilutive 0%-coupon converts with a term loan — reduces long-run share-dilution risk, at the cost of new cash interest
Duopoly position intact — DraftKings + FanDuel = 78% of US sports-betting GGR, and both shrank by similar amounts in Q2 (confirms the pressure is sector-wide, not share loss to each other)
MUPs (monthly unique players) still growing +9% YoY to 3.6M — the player base isn't shrinking, only revenue-per-player is

Risks

NEW (25 Sep 2026): even after Needham adjusted NFL Week 1 data to strip out professional repeat-traders (narrowing Kalshi's share from 76% to 67%), DKeX itself barely moved from its ~3% baseline — DraftKings' own 'near-double-digit' share claim (22 Sep) is not yet corroborated by independent data
NEW (23 Sep 2026): management will pull forward 2027 prediction-market promo spend into 2026 — analysts flagged this as margin-negative in the near term regardless of long-run payoff; stock fell 4% on the announcement
NEW (25 Aug 2026): refinanced $700M into a new Term Loan B (SOFR+2%, 2033) to repurchase converts, plus upsized the revolver to $750M — adds ~$40–45M/yr of real cash interest on top of already-negative FCF; the balance-sheet strength that used to be DKNG's clearest edge over FLUT is now weaker
S&M spend already running hotter than expected for the NFL season — 22.3% of Q2 revenue vs 15.4% a year ago — and now set to rise further per the pull-forward plan
Circuit split still unresolved nationally — Third Circuit (Apr 2026) and Ninth Circuit (Aug 2026) sided with states/sportsbooks, Connecticut (Aug 2026) ruled the opposite way; SCOTUS cert petitions from Robinhood/Crypto.com are docketed but not yet scheduled
3 consumer lawsuits alleging DKNG's own Predictions product is an unlicensed sportsbook remain unresolved, no material progress since filing in late Jul/early Aug 2026
Permanent state tax increases already landed (NJ 14.25%→21%, LA 15%→21.5%, MD 15%→20%) — these don't reverse when the promo cycle normalizes
Negative free cash flow (H1'26 −$24M, now with added cash interest from the Aug refinancing) while still trading at a full multiple on the FY26 EBITDA guide; Q3 due 5 Nov 2026
