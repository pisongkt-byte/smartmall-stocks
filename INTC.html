Risks

Node & customer execution — 18A/14A must hit yield AND win external customers; external foundry revenue is still only ~$0.7B/yr and no anchor logic customer has firmly committed

Dilution — share count up ~17% (≈4.3B→5.0B) from govt/Nvidia/SoftBank/escrow; further capital raises likely if foundry burn persists
