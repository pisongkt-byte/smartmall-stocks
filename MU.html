Rewards & Risks

Rewards

FQ3-26 was the best quarter any memory company has ever reported: revenue $41.46B +346% YoY, gross margin 84.6%, net income $28.24B — verified against the 10-Q on EDGAR, not aggregator data
FQ4-26 guided even higher: $50B ±$1B at ~86% GM — the only one of the big-3 memory names still guiding +21% QoQ growth
$100B in minimum contracted, take-or-pay revenue (16 agreements, CY26–30, ~$18B cash deposits in hand) — a structural cycle-dampener no memory maker has ever had before
HBM4 shipping in high volume to Nvidia's Rubin platform ahead of Samsung's qualification; HBM4E on track CY2027 — Micron has leapfrogged Samsung into the #2 HBM technology slot
Net cash ~$24B + 9M FCF ~$26B + CHIPS Act backing (up to $6.4B grants + 35% ITC) — best-funded US memory expansion in history
Thai DR access confirmed: MICRON01 (฿19.90), MICRON80 (฿31.75), MICRON23 (฿6.35) — buyable in baht, no offshore account needed

Risks

CYCLE-TOP SIGNATURE — server DRAM contract-price growth has decelerated three straight quarters (+90-95% → +58-63% → +13-18% QoQ); 85% gross margin is DOUBLE any prior cycle peak, and FY23's trough was a −$5.8B loss. Memory cycles have never not mean-reverted
CXMT (ChangXin) blockbuster IPO late July + reports Apple is testing CXMT DRAM for China devices — the proximate cause of the −32% four-week drawdown; Chinese commodity-DRAM displacement pushes everyone's bits into the rest of the market in 2027
The $100B contract floor is ~$20B/yr — only ~15% of the FY26 revenue run-rate. Take-or-pay protects volumes at minimums, not today's spot-inflated pricing; a normal downcycle still cuts EPS 50-70%
P/B 9.2x vs ~1x at prior troughs — no asset-value floor if earnings normalize; bear-case scenario ($350-500) is a further −40-55% from here
AI capex digestion: hyperscaler budgets flattening in 2027 would compress the premium above contracted minimums right as MU/SK/Samsung HBM capacity additions all land
