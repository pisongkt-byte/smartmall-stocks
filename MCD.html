Rewards & Risks

Rewards

Cheapest MCD has been in a decade ex-COVID: 19.4x trailing vs its own 2021-25 band of 24-25x, and a 3.24% yield vs a 5-year average of 2.24%
50 consecutive years of dividend increases — officially a Dividend King as of 17 Sep 2026 — covered 1.42x by $7.76B of free cash flow
Operating margin 46.2% is 14pp above the next-best QSR (YUM 32.8%), yet the stock trades at a similar P/E — quality bought at an average-quality price
95% franchised with MCD owning or long-leasing the land and buildings: this is a real-estate annuity with a 20-year contract life, not a burger P&L
Loyalty is the underrated asset: ~220M 90-day active members across 70 markets driving $40B of systemwide sales (+20% TTM) — the foundation for both personalisation and the new retail media network
International Operated Markets now out-earn the US ($1,723M vs $1,573M of quarterly operating income) and are growing faster — the problem is one segment, not the whole company
Beta 0.41 and USD earnings from 100+ countries — exactly the exposure a 100%-China, zero-cash portfolio does not have

Risks

U.S. comparable guest counts have been negative for two straight years and the 10-Q confirms Q2-26 comps of +0.8% came from 'positive check growth... partly offset by negative comparable guest counts' — growth is pure price. You cannot price your way out of a traffic problem forever, and there is still no confirmed inflection.
The $8.5B NEXT partnering support (rent relief + capital, through 2036, ~$5B by 2030) is a margin give-back with an unknown endpoint — it lands on the 84%-margin franchised occupancy line and is an implicit admission franchisee economics are strained. Citi cut its target to $310 from $345 citing doubts about franchisee buy-in.
Interest expense is compounding faster than profit: +5% YoY in Q2-26 against +3% operating income, on $39.9B of long-term debt plus $14.0B of lease liability against $0.82B of cash. Every refinancing at a higher coupon is a permanent EPS drag.
Dividend growth is decelerating every year (+9.9% → +6.0% → +5.1% → +3.8%). The dividend is safe; the growth rate is the tell that NEXT is consuming more cash than management is saying out loud. A sub-3% raise in Sep 2027 would be a red flag.
Management transition in the worst place at the worst time: Joe Erlinger stepped down as President of McDonald's USA on 4 Aug 2026 (8-K item 5.02), replaced by 26-year insider Skye Anderson — mid-crisis in the single largest market.
IDL & Corporate operating income collapsed to $42M from $70M (−40% YoY) with China comps negative. MCD holds a 48% equity-method stake in McDonald's China (~5,500 stores) acquired from Carlyle in 2024.
GLP-1 is a slow structural tax, not a bomb: households with a GLP-1 user cut QSR/coffee/limited-service spending ~8.6%, and regular users cut dinner visits 6% — but industry-wide that is only ~0.4% of dinner sales, and even a doubling of usage over five years would cost the industry under 1% of sales growth. MCD is responding by reformulating 17 menu items toward protein and targeting +1.5pp of chicken share by 2030.
