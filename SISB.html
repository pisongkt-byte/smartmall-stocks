Rewards & Risks

Rewards

#1 international-school operator in Thailand by students (~5.5% share, ahead of Nord Anglia 5.3%) & only SET pure-play
Penetration runway: TH international-school penetration ~0.8% vs Malaysia ~2.0% — sector students grew 50k→93k (2014→2025) even as Thai birth rate fell
Demographics offset: ~21% of students are Chinese (72% of foreign) — relocation demand decoupled from Thai fertility; primary+secondary +3% in 2025
Extreme switching costs (~฿590k/yr, parents rarely move kids) + 70–80% fixed-cost base = operating leverage once capacity refills
Trailing P/E ~9.1x & EV/EBITDA ~6.3x (well under half its 20–25x history) + ~5.8% yield on ~50-54% payout — price has drifted into the pre-set ฿8.50–9.20 re-entry zone

Risks

Growth genuinely STALLED — 3 straight quarters of net-profit decline (1Q26 −9.28%, 2Q26 −10.48%, H1 −9.87%); Krungsri's 02-Sep-26 note confirms mgmt guides FY26 NP -8% YoY on a flat-to-down enrollment year. The de-rating is partly earned, though the real guide is lighter than headline-only readings implied
