Rewards & Risks

Rewards

Absolute EUV monopoly — the sole supplier of extreme-ultraviolet lithography, the only path to sub-7nm chips; High-NA EUV extends the moat another decade
Q2 2026: €9.3B net sales (+20.8% YoY), €2.9B net income (31.3% margin), gross margin 54.0% (beat guide)
FY2026 guidance raised twice to €43-45B (~+34% YoY)

Every leading-edge fab (TSMC, Samsung, Intel, SK Hynix, Micron) must buy from ASML — pricing power + a 30%-of-revenue recurring service annuity

Risks

MATCH Act (US) — escalated further (Sep 2026): now reportedly bundled with the AI OVERWATCH Act and Chip Security Act as a package inside the Senate NDAA manager's amendment, not a standalone bill. Covers servicing of ~1,400 already-installed DUV tools in China, with a 150-day Dutch compliance deadline before automatic Foreign Direct Product Rule expansion. First quantified impact estimate (TechTimes, 9 Sep 2026): a full sales+servicing ban would cut revenue ~14-15% and operating profit ~16-17% — though China's CXMT/YMTC have ~3 years of DUV inventory stockpiled, easing near-term sales urgency and shifting the real lever to servicing (needed every ~6 months)
Valuation — ~13% off the $1,989.44 52wk high at ~53x trailing / ~37x FY26e; price ran up ~3.7% on a sector-wide chipmaker rally with no ASML-specific news, narrowing the margin of safety further; priced for perfection, any demand wobble de-rates hard
Order-book opacity — ASML stopped disclosing quarterly bookings from Q1 2026, reducing forward visibility despite a €38.8B backlog
Customer concentration + cyclicality — a handful of buyers (TSMC, Samsung, Intel); Intel's foundry struggles or any single customer's capex cut swings orders
